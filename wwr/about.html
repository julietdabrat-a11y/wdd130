Everest Whitewater Expeditions

Our mission is to deliver safe, high-energy whitewater rafting trips that connect guests with wild rivers and build lasting memories. Every trip is led by trained river guides focused on safety, teamwork, and fun.

History

Founded with a passion for river exploration, Everest Whitewater Expeditions started with just a handful of rafts and guides eager to share rushing mountain waters with adventurous travelers.

Over the years, we have grown into a trusted river outfitter guiding families and thrill-seekers across scenic canyons and exciting rapids while protecting local waterways.
